Privacy policy

How Green Analytics Group handles personal information on this website, including our AI assistant, and the rights you have over it.

Last updated10 September 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Green Analytics Group (“we”, “us”), the Canadian parent of Green Analytics Consulting Corp., Green Metrics Technology Corp., Precision Livestock Diagnostics Ltd., the Wolastoqey Forest Partnership, Open Housing Canada and Impact Natural Capital Corp. We are responsible for any personal information collected through this site. Questions about this policy, or requests about your information, go to our privacy officer at info@greenanalytics.ca.

We comply with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the substantially similar provincial laws in British Columbia, Alberta and Quebec (including Quebec’s Law 25), Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), and — for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the GDPR and UK GDPR.

The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising trackers, and loads no fonts, scripts or images from third-party servers. The only information that leaves your browser is what you choose to type into the AI assistant, and the ordinary technical data any web server receives when you load a page.

What we collect, and why

1. The AI assistant (“Ask our AI”)

If you open the assistant and ask a question, the text of your question — together with the earlier messages in that conversation, so the assistant can follow context — is sent to our knowledge service, which retrieves matching passages from our public corpus and asks a large language model to write a reply. That model is provided by Anthropic, PBC, in the United States, and processes your messages under Anthropic’s commercial terms, which do not permit use of your inputs to train its models.

  • We do not ask for, and do not need, your name, email address or any other personal information to use the assistant. Please don’t include personal information in your questions.
  • We do not store your conversation in a database. It lives in your browser only until you close or reload the page. Our servers and Anthropic may keep short-lived operational logs, which we do not use to identify anyone.
  • The assistant only ever draws on material we have already published. It has no access to client, project or internal information.
  • Answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. Verify anything important with us directly.

Legal basis: your consent, given by choosing to type a question after seeing the notice in the assistant panel. You can withdraw it at any time by simply not using the assistant.

2. Technical data received by our web server

Like every website, when you load a page our hosting provider’s servers receive your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep these in short-term server logs used for security and to keep the site running. We do not use them for analytics, profiling or advertising, and we do not combine them with any other information about you.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure website.

3. Email you send us

If you email info@greenanalytics.ca, we use what you send us only to reply and, if you become a client or partner, to manage that relationship. Messages are handled in our Microsoft 365 business mailbox and kept for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless they form part of a client file.

What we do not do

  • We do not use cookies, local storage for tracking, advertising pixels or browser fingerprinting.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics service.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties for their own purposes.
  • We do not send commercial electronic messages without your express consent, and every message we send includes a way to unsubscribe, as CASL requires.
  • We do not load fonts, scripts, video or images from other companies’ servers, so no data about your visit is transmitted to them.

Where information is processed

This website and our knowledge service are hosted on infrastructure operated by Railway Corporation in the United States. Questions you ask the assistant are processed by Anthropic, PBC, also in the United States. Information may therefore be processed outside Canada and outside the EEA/UK, and could be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction. Transfers from the EEA/UK rely on the providers’ standard contractual clauses.

Links to our companies’ websites

This site links to the websites of our operating companies (for example greenanalytics.ca, greenmetrics.ca, openhousingcanada.ca and wfcp.ca). Each of those sites is governed by its own privacy policy, which applies once you leave this site.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to give you a copy in a portable format, to stop using it, or to withdraw a consent you gave earlier. Write to info@greenanalytics.ca. We respond within 30 days and do not charge a fee. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, your provincial commissioner, or — in the EEA or UK — your local supervisory authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. The assistant can only reach our public corpus; access to anything else is capped on the server side, regardless of what a request asks for. We keep the number of people with administrative access small and review it periodically.

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA on this site. If anything here is difficult for you to use, tell us at info@greenanalytics.ca and we will fix it or provide the content another way.

Children

This site is intended for professionals and organisations. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is material, note it on this page for at least 30 days.
